HealthKit Data Generator is a powerful tool for developers and QA engineers to populate Apple HealthKit with realistic sample health data. Perfect for screenshots, demos, UI testing, and quality assurance.

KEY FEATURES

Manual Data Generation
- Choose from preset health profiles: Athletic, Balanced, Stressed, or Sedentary
- Customize date ranges and generation patterns
- Fine-grained control over which health metrics to generate
- Advanced pattern settings including continuous, sparse, and custom probability modes

AI-Powered Generation
- Describe the health data you need in plain English
- Powered by Apple Foundation Models
- Example: "Create 2 weeks of marathon training data for an active athlete"

18 Health Metrics Supported
- Steps, Heart Rate, Heart Rate Variability
- Sleep Analysis, Workouts, Active & Basal Energy
- Blood Pressure, Blood Glucose, Body Mass
- Body Fat, Lean Body Mass, Water Intake
- Mindful Minutes, Respiratory Rate, Oxygen Saturation
- Dietary Tracking: Sugar, Protein, Carbs, Fat

Data Management
- Export HealthKit data to JSON
- Import health data from JSON profiles
- Clean up generated test data with one tap

10 Workout Types
Running, Cycling, Swimming, Walking, Yoga, Strength Training, HIIT, Pilates, Dancing, and Sports

Built for developers who need realistic HealthKit data without manually entering hundreds of data points. Seed your HealthKit store in minutes, not hours.
